📋 How PIIMa Works

Medical documents often contain names, patient numbers, and contact details. These details create risks if shared outside of secure environments. PIIMa scans your text files and masks this sensitive information. The software currently targets structured identifiers to prevent identity theft and privacy leaks.

🔒 Why Privacy Matters

Healthcare privacy remains a priority for every medical facility. HIPAA and KVKK regulations demand strict control over patient data. PIIMa acts as a filter for your clinical records. By removing identifiers before you store or share data, you reduce the risk of accidental exposure. We build our pipeline to verify data integrity without storing any personal files on external servers.

💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Does this software send my data to the internet? No. PIIMa processes your medical documents on your own computer. Your data stays on your machine at all times.

Is it hard to use? We keep the interface simple. You open the application, select your folder of documents, and click the process button.

What file formats does it support? PIIMa currently supports standard text files and structured clinical reports.

Note: This software is in a pre-alpha stage. It masks structured identifiers like patient ID numbers or registration codes. It does not yet mask person names. Always review your output files to ensure complete privacy before sharing them.
